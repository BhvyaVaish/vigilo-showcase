# 3-Minute Live Demonstration Script

This document provides a structured, live-demonstration walkthrough designed for presentations at **Google DevFest Lucknow**, product showcase evaluations, and technical interviews.

**Total Duration:** 3 minutes (180 seconds)  
**Safety Rule:** Never trigger an actual emergency call or contact municipal emergency services during this demonstration.

---

## Demonstration Overview Timeline

```
[0:00 - 0:30] Problem & Context
[0:30 - 1:00] Launching Vigilo & Starting Cockpit
[1:00 - 1:45] Triggering Simulated Impact & Alert Countdown
[1:45 - 2:30] Evidence Vault & Deterministic Report
[2:30 - 3:00] Architectural Depth & Closing
```

---

## Step-by-Step Script

### Stage 1: The Problem Space (0:00 – 0:30 | 30 seconds)
* **What to Say:**
  > *"When a serious road crash happens, victims are often concussed, injured, or trapped—unable to unlock their phone or call for help. Existing dashcams record passively, and generic SOS buttons require conscious human action. Vigilo solves this with an autonomous, phone-only telematics system that detects impact, verifies driver consciousness, dispatches location, and seals forensic evidence."*
* **What to Show:** Present the Vigilo application launch screen.

---

### Stage 2: Starting Drive Mode & Live Cockpit (0:30 – 1:00 | 30 seconds)
* **What to Do:**
  1. From the home screen, tap **"Start Drive Mode"**.
  2. Select the **`Car Mount`** profile (pointing out that thresholds adapt to mounting placement).
  3. Tap **"Begin Drive Monitoring"**.
* **What to Say:**
  > *"Vigilo initializes an Android Foreground Service sampling accelerometer and gyroscope data at 50Hz. On the active cockpit screen, you see real-time G-force, GPS speed, and our 9-dimensional sensor fusion engine actively monitoring driving dynamics in the background."*
* **What to Show:** Point to the real-time G-force gauge (~1.0G at rest) and the GPS locked indicator.

---

### Stage 3: Simulated Impact & Zero-Touch Escalation (1:00 – 1:45 | 45 seconds)
* **What to Do:**
  1. In the drive cockpit, tap **"Test Safety Alert Flow"**.
  2. The screen pulses red with an audible countdown from 10 seconds.
  3. Point out the **Confidence Tier (HIGH)** and the plain-language explanation.
  4. Demonstrate hands-free voice command: Speak *"I'm okay"* or tap **"False Alarm"** to show instant cancellation.
  5. Retrigger and let the countdown reach zero, or tap **"I Need Help"** to show autonomous escalation.
* **What to Say:**
  > *"Upon impact detection, Vigilo gives the driver a 10-second verification window. The driver can cancel verbally by saying 'I'm okay' or tapping the screen. If there is no response, Vigilo assumes incapacitation: it dispatches background SMS alerts with exact GPS coordinates and Google Maps links to emergency contacts, and activates Bystander Mode with a strobing flashlight."*
* **What to Show:** Show the Bystander SOS screen with strobing torch and the Emergency Assistance screen.

---

### Stage 4: Evidence Vault & Deterministic Reporting (1:45 – 2:30 | 45 seconds)
* **What to Do:**
  1. Navigate to **Incident History** -> Open the newly recorded incident.
  2. Tap **"Verify All Evidence"**.
  3. Point out the green **`VERIFIED`** badge.
  4. Tap **"View Factual Report"** and toggle between Text and JSON formats.
* **What to Say:**
  > *"Every incident is sealed using the Android Hardware Keystore. The system signs the exact telemetry facts and video SHA-256 hash with an ECDSA private key inside the phone's secure enclave. When we tap 'Verify', it proves the record has never been tampered with. We can then export a deterministic factual report for insurance or police review, alongside an advisory AI narrative generated by Gemini."*
* **What to Show:** Display the cryptographic verification status and the structured JSON report preview.

---

### Stage 5: Architecture & Closing (2:30 – 3:00 | 30 seconds)
* **What to Say:**
  > *"Vigilo is 100% local-first and offline-capable. It doesn't rely on the cloud for basic life safety. It combines low-level Android sensor fusion, on-device TFLite machine learning, hardware security, and Gemini AI. Thank you."*

## Fallback Plan & Contingencies

| Potential Failure Point | Root Cause | Fallback Action During Live Demo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **No GPS Fix Inside Building** | Concrete shielding blocks satellite signals. | Vigilo gracefully falls back to coarse location or last known fix; the UI displays a `Degraded State` flag without crashing. Point this out as proof of resilient software architecture! |
| **Speech Recognizer Fails in Noisy Room** | Background auditorium noise drowns out voice command. | Tap the physical **"I'm OK"** or **"False Alarm"** button on screen. Explain: *"The system offers multi-modal cancellation—both voice and touch."* |
| **No Cellular Signal for SMS** | Basement demo area lacks carrier reception. | Show that the incident is preserved locally in the Room database, ready for auto-sync when cellular signal returns. |
| **Gemini AI API Network Timeout** | WiFi drops during report generation. | Vigilo's `AiRepositoryImpl` displays its built-in offline fallback notice: *(API unavailable — offline mode)*. The deterministic facts remain 100% complete. |
