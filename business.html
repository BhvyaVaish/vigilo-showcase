Enterprise Solutions

Three integration pathways for organizations operating in road safety, fleet operations, or mobility insurance.

Fleet Safety Integration

SDK-level integration for fleet management platforms. Real-time incident alerting, driver safety scoring, and compliance-ready documentation. Works alongside existing telematics infrastructure — no additional hardware required.

Real-time incident alerts to fleet managers
Per-driver safety profiles and incident history

Insurance Technology

IRDAI-compatible incident exports with SHA-256 verified evidence. Structured JSON reports designed for automated claims processing. Reduces manual documentation burden on policyholders and adjusters.

SHA-256 cryptographically signed incident records
JSON export for automated claims ingestion
